# CONSTITUTION

**Imperial Archive — Foundational Constitution**

---

## Article 1: The Origin is Sacred

The founding story, the founder's imprint, and the original intent
are non-commodifiable and cannot be rewritten.

## Article 2: The Child is Protected

No child data leaves the designated folder.
No child is ever exposed.

## Article 3: The Vision Leads

Technology serves vision, never the reverse.

## Article 4: The Founder Decides

The founder holds final authority on all matters.

## Article 5: The System Persists

The system survives founder absence through governance and recovery.

## Article 6: The Legacy Lives

Imperial Archive must remain productive, generative, adaptive.
It is not a museum. It is a living system.
